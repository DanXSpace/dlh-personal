# Mentor feedback revisions

## Problem

A mentor review (2026-11-25) found that the homepage didn't say plainly what the owner does, put a large photo between the introduction and the work on phones, and showed thin project rows. It also found that the résumé only downloaded instead of opening in the browser.

## Goal

The homepage says who the owner is, leads with the work, and makes the résumé easy to read. All of it stays within the Quiet Precision design direction (`docs/design-spec.md`) and the content-integrity rules in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Tickets

- `issues/01-homepage-and-resume-revisions.md`: hero title, mobile landscape strip, Selected work rows, three featured projects, the inline résumé viewer, and a résumé PDF without the phone number.
- `issues/02-personal-website-case-study.md`: a full case study for this website, replacing the draft placeholder from ticket 01.

## Decisions

The owner approved every change. Each ticket's Comments section records the revisions made to keep the design direction.
