My interest in technology grew out of an interest in financial markets.

Before I was writing software, I was studying economics, following markets and trying to understand the systems that shape the world around us. That curiosity led me from economics into finance, and eventually into engineering.

My professional background is in data engineering, working with data pipelines, distributed services and financial information. Outside of work, I enjoy building software that reflects my own interests and solves problems I encounter in everyday life.

I'm particularly interested in software architecture, thoughtful product design and the relationship between complexity and simplicity.

This website is a place to bring those interests together.
